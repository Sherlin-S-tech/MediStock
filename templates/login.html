MEDICINE INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

Stock clarity for every batch.

MediStock helps administrators organize medicine batches, monitor expiry dates, and keep an eye on stock levels from one simple workspace.

About MediStock

A responsive inventory application built with Flask and SQLite, with a delivery workflow using pytest, Jenkins, Docker, and Ansible.

Application features

  • ✓
    Batch inventorySearch, review, add, edit, and remove medicine records.
  • ◷
    Expiry monitoringGroup batches into expired, near-expiry, and valid stock.
  • ⚑
    Low-stock alertsFind batches at or below their configured reorder threshold.
  • ▧
    Reports and healthDownload CSV reports and check service readiness.
